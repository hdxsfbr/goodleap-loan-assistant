Once pay frequency and gross pay are confirmed, the system computes the annualized income: sixty-one thousand one hundred dollars, against eighty-five thousand stated on the application.

That is outside tolerance, so a discrepancy record opens with a question written from a template for that reason code, never by the model. The reviewer can edit it and sends it. The contractor sees it as a banner on the application and replies. The reviewer resolves the record and completes the review. The stated value itself changes only through the portal's existing process.
